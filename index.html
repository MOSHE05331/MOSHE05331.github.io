Coach Desk (שולחן המאמן) – in English

שולחן המאמן ("Coach Desk") is a free desktop application for personal coaches. It manages coaching sessions, session recordings and summaries, tasks, goals and worksheets, client reminders (email, SMS, WhatsApp), a self-booking page and a secure personal area for each client.

Google Account connection (optional): the coach can connect their own Google Account so that the App can (1) add the sessions the coach schedules to their Google Calendar and check busy times to avoid double booking, (2) send reminders, confirmations and summaries to the coach's clients from the coach's own Gmail address, and (3) back up the App's data, and optionally encrypted session recordings, to the coach's Google Drive. All data stays with the coach – on their computer or their own server. The developer has no access to it, and Google user data is never used for advertising.
